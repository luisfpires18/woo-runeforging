## Nordalh

**Capital:** Haven
**Geography:** Fjords, green belts, hot springs, ice fields, wind-beaten coasts, pine woods
**Core Identity:** A northern clan realm of hard winters, smithing pride, brutal melee, direwolf cavalry, and cold-weather endurance.
**Visual Identity:** Wolfpelt mantles, riveted mail, scale plates, frost-blue cloth, carved iron buckles, smith-marked armor, heavy furs, chain and iron weaponry.
**Playstyle:** Brutal melee, shield pressure, axe aggression, cold-weather ranged support, beast cavalry, and forge-crafted northern elites.

---

## Buildings

### 1. Barracks

* **Shieldmaiden**
  Weapon: Dual-Wield Shields
  Role: Ultimate defense, shield-bash offense, frontline anchor

* **Viking**
  Weapon: Axe + Shield
  Role: Core melee fighter, aggressive frontline, raider

* **Barbarian**
  Weapon: Two-Handed Bone Club
  Role: Heavy shock infantry, crushing melee, brute force

* **Berserker**
  Weapon: Dual-Wield Greataxes
  Role: Elite fury infantry, line breaker, savage assault

### 2. Archery

* **Poacher**

* **Hunter**
  Weapon: Frost Crossbow
  Role: Armor-piercing ranged support, cold pressure

* **Pathfinder**
  Weapon: Bow
