## Veridor

**Capital:** Azure
**Geography:** Northern highlands, foothill forests, river valleys, fortified trade roads, southern deep-water ports, coastal plains, and island waters
**Core Identity:** A wealthy trade kingdom split between inland road soldiery and southern naval power — disciplined, expansionist, practical, and built on commerce, logistics, ports, and force projection. 
**Visual Identity:** North Veridor uses riverroad brigandine with cobalt sashes; South Veridor uses saltcured leather coats, brass buckles, sailor scarves, and marine gear. 
**Playstyle:** Mixed kingdom of road infantry, naval assault troops, covert operatives, ranged pressure, cavalry patrols, and water-mounted units. North Veridor carries the inland army and fortified roads; South Veridor carries the navy, shipyards, and hydra patrols.
