## Draxys

**Capital:** Scorcheye
**Geography:** Oases, rocky desert, canyon belts, sun-scorched plains, defended settlement cores
**Core Identity:** A desert frontier kingdom forged by survival, spectacle, and brutality — arena-trained fighters, venom warfare, beast riders, and harsh control of water and land.
**Visual Identity:** Sandstone armor, wrapped cloth, bronze fittings, scale plates, hooked blades, venom vials, hide mantles, sun-bleached leather, scorpion motifs.
**Playstyle:** Arena fighters, poison pressure, beast riders, anti-cavalry tricks, desert skirmishing, shock assaults.

---

## Buildings

### 1. Barracks

* **Gladiator**
  Weapon: Sword + Large Shield
  Role: Heavy arena fighter, durable frontline, crowd-control melee

* **Bladedancer**
  Weapon: Dual-Wield Swords
  Role: Agile arena fighter, fast melee pressure, anti-light

* **Rogue**
  Weapon: Dual-Wield Daggers
  Role: Venom assassin, backline pressure, fast kill unit

* **Myrmidon**
  Weapon: Spear + Shield
  Role: Reach control, anti-charge, formation fighter

* **Netfighter**
  Weapon: Trident + Net
  Role: Control unit, entangle, anti-large, anti-cavalry

### 2. Archery

* **Nomad**
  Weapon: Bow
  Role: Main ranged unit, desert pressure, standard ranged support

* **Venomblade**
  Weapon: Throwing Venom Knives
  Role: Fast ranged pressure, poison stacking, anti-light

* **Sandwraith**
  Weapon: Chakrams
  Role: Fast slicing ranged unit, mobile pressure, anti-light, flashy arena fighter

### 3. Stable

* **Outrider**
  Weapon: Bow
  Mount: Camel
  Role: Fast desert cavalry, ranged harassment, scouting, pursuit

* **Lashmaster**
  Weapon: Dual Whips
  Mount: Scorpion
  Role: Venom cavalry, disruption, anti-light, control

* **Dreadstinger**
