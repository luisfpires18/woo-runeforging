## Lumus

**Capital:** Sol
**Geography:** A radiant island realm of white-stone walls, temple courts, bright coasts, high terraces, and disciplined settlements built around sacred order and solar tradition.
**Core Identity:** A holy island kingdom of temple warriors, martial schools, disciplined archers, and elite cavalry — precise, ordered, and built on faith, training, and battlefield control.
**Visual Identity:** White plate, pale gold trim, sun emblems, polished shields, wrapped hands, long staves, ceremonial cloaks, bright leather tack, and clean holy silhouettes.
**Playstyle:** Defensive frontlines, martial melee pressure, holy heavy infantry, disciplined ranged support, and elite cavalry with strong battlefield presence.

---

## Buildings

### 1. Barracks

* **Templar**
  Weapon: Sword + Shield
  Role: Core line infantry, defensive frontline, formation anchor

* **Martial Artist**
  Weapon: Pummelers
  Role: Fast melee pressure, anti-light, combo striker

* **Paladin**
  Weapon: Great Hammer Two-Handed
  Role: Heavy holy infantry, shock melee, armor breaker

* **Monk**
  Weapon: Iron Staff
  Role: Reach melee, sweeping control, anti-skirmisher

### 2. Archery

* **Sentinel**
  Weapon: Bow
  Role: Main ranged unit, disciplined long-range support

* **Zealot**
  Weapon: Dual-Wield Chakrams
  Role: Fast ranged pressure, anti-light, mobile burst fire

* **Inquisitor**
  Weapon: Divine Boomerang
  Role: Disruptive ranged support, ricochet pressure, utility harassment

### 3. Stable

* **Crusader**
  Weapon: Great Hammer
  Mount: Warhorse
  Role: Core holy cavalry, shock charge, breakthrough

* **Marshal**
  Weapon: Dual-Wield Swords
  Mount: Warhorse
  Role: Elite cavalry, pursuit, flanking kill unit

* **Justicar**
  Weapon: War Staff
  Mount: Warhorse
  Role: Control cavalry, disciplined disruption, support rider

This version keeps the medieval feel, avoids reused generic names, and gives Lumus a distinct identity around holy order plus martial discipline.
