## Zandres

**Capital:** Deephearth
**Geography:** Vast underground holds, mining roads, crystal caverns, iron veins, obsidian chambers, sulfur pits, forge halls, and beast tunnels beneath the mountains.
**Core Identity:** An underground human hold kingdom shaped by mining, engineering, tunnel warfare, and crushing heavy infantry — disciplined, industrial, and built to dominate tight ground.
**Visual Identity:** Iron plate, obsidian-edged weapons, miner lanterns, heavy leather, forge masks, riveted armor, stonework crests, sulfur flasks, and hard subterranean silhouettes.
**Playstyle:** Heavy frontline pressure, demolition warfare, armor-piercing ranged support, engineered battlefield control, and beast-mounted underground cavalry.
