# Sylvara

**Capital:** Unitas  
**Geography:** Deep forests, herb meadows, river cutbanks, old-growth clearings, inland settlement network  
**Core Identity:** The original woodland heartland of humanity — disciplined, self-sufficient, nature-bound, rich in lumber, herbs, and game, with strong archery, beast mastery, and healing traditions.  
**Visual Identity:** carved bark armor, heartwood plates, leather underlayers, leaf-pattern shields, vine-laced brigandines, minimal steel.  
**Playstyle:** forest warfare, archery, ambushes, scouting, beast bonds, crafted wood armor.
