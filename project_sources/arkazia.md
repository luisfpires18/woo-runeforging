## Arkazia

**Capital:** Obsidia
**Geography:** Mountain strongholds, fortified passes, stone-built towns, harsh ridgelands, alpine forests, iron-rich slopes
**Core Identity:** A hard mountain war realm forged by discipline, fortresses, steel, and elite cavalry — built to hold ground, break enemy lines, and survive brutal border warfare.
**Visual Identity:** Blackened steel armor, crimson cloth, full helms, iron kite shields, riveted plate, heavy cavalry barding, fortress stonework, forge-smoke militarism.
**Playstyle:** Fortress warfare, shield lines, pike control, heavy infantry, elite cavalry, disciplined aggression, strong anti-cavalry tools.

---

## Buildings

### 1. Barracks: Red Bastion

* **Bastion**
  Weapon: Sword + Shield
  Role: Core line infantry, defensive frontline

* **Phalanx**
  Weapon: Pike Two-Handed
  Role: Anti-cavalry, formation control, pass holder

* **Duelist**
  Weapon: Swords Dual-Wield
  Role: Fast melee specialist, duels, aggressive flanker

* **Warrior**
  Weapon: Sword Two-Handed
  Role: Heavy melee, shock infantry, armor breaker

### 2. Archery: Ridge Range

* **Hatchetier**
  Weapon: Throwing Axes
  Role: Mid-range pressure, anti-light, rugged skirmish support

* **Marksman**
  Weapon: Bow Two-Handed
  Role: Main ranged unit, disciplined long-range support

* **Vindicator**
  Weapon: Dual Wield Throwing Shields
  Role: Short-range ranged pressure, anti-light, disruptive support

* **Mauler**
